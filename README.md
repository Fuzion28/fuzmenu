# 🧟 FuzMenu v2.4.5: ALL FEATURES 🧟

Zombies mod menu for BO7 / Kronos, solo and **co-op**.
Open: **Aim + Melee** · Move: Aim / Shoot or arrows · Select: **F** · Back: **V**

## 🛡️ OP Mode
One switch turns on god mode, infinite ammo & points, free purchases, aimbot, wallhack, rapid fire, no recoil, Ultra + PaP 4, speed, jump x4, low gravity and all perks.

## 🧍 Player
- God mode, Zombies ignore me, Cinematic mode
- Noclip (also the **N** key)
- Infinite points, Free purchases
- Speed x1.6, Infinite tactical sprint
- No fall damage, No explosive self-damage
- Restore health, Self revives x10
- Jump height (x1.5 to x4), Gravity (x0.25 to x2)
- Armor: 1 / 2 / 3 plates, Golden armor, Back shield (15 hits)

## 📍 Teleports
Map locations, Save / Load position, Return to spawn, Unstuck

## 🔫 Give a weapon
- Every weapon class: assault rifles, SMGs, LMGs, shotguns, marksman, snipers, pistols, launchers, melee
- My loadout primary / secondary, Random weapon, Remove held weapon
- Equipment: Kazimir, Decoy, Monkey bomb, Stim, Stun, Flash, Smoke, Frag, Semtex, Molotov, Cluster, Throwing knife, Combat axe, C4, Claymore

## ✨ Wonder weapons
Necrofluid, Nekomancer, Jotunn Star, Blundergat, Sundergat, LGM-1, Warden's Blight, Ray Gun, Ray Gun Mk2

## 🎯 Shooting options
Infinite ammo, Rapid fire, Fast reload, No recoil, Free wall ammo, Aimbot (while aiming), Wallhack (zombies), Infinite equipment, Infinite field upgrade, Unlimited weapons, Refill weapons

## 💥 Damage
- Weapon damage x2 to x100, Critical damage x2 to x25, Melee damage x2 to x100
- Ammo mods: Brain Rot, Cryo Freeze, Dead Wire, Napalm Burst, Shadow Rift, Shatter Blast, Light Mend

## ⬆️ Upgrade
- Pack-a-Punch I to IV, Free PaP
- Weapon rarity: Common to Ultra
- Auto Ultra + PaP 4, Random camo
- **Saved builds**: 10 slots, kept even after a game restart

## 🥤 Perks
All perks, Eternal perks, Pick a perk, Remove all perks, Free perks

## 🍬 Gobblegums
Random gums, every gum by rarity (Rare, Epic, Legendary, Ultra, Whimsical): equip it in a free slot or use it now

## 💰 Points & scrap
Max points & scrap, Add points / scrap, Points multiplier x2 to x10, Scrap multiplier x2 to x10, Free crafting

## 🧟 Zombies
- Kill all, Leave one zombie, Despawn zombies, Stop spawns, Freeze zombies
- Zombies per round (1 to 24), Spawn rate x0.5 to x4, Force spawn here
- **Spawn points**: place as many as you want, zombies spawn at a random one
- Zombies in a line (training), Zombie counter
- Friendly zombies, Tessie

## ⏱️ Rounds
Round timer, Round +1 / +5 / +10 / +100, Round -1 / -5 / -10 / -100, Jump to round 1, 10, 25, 50, 100, 255, 500 or 999

## 🏃 Speedrun
- Live timer and splits, personal best saved, the timer starts when you move
- **Ashes**: Gauntlet, Lockdown end, Keys, Challenges, Veytarion
- **Astra**: LGM-1, Brain, Mars, Ritual, Caltheris
- Other maps: PaP, Wonder weapon, each main quest step, Boss
- **Practice**: replay any split with the quest set up, round, perks, armor, points and PaP ready
- Save your own start position for each split

## 🎁 Bonus
All power-ups, Double points, Max ammo, Max armor, Full power, Free perk, Fire sale, Permanent fire sale, Nuke, Bonus points, Power-up duration x2 / x5

## 🌍 World
Turn on power, Open all doors, **Start Boss Fight** (all maps), Restart map, Disable all mods
Mystery box: Free, Faster, Lock the box in place

## 🔨 Forge
- **Props**: Box, Door, Wall, Tower, Stairs, Mini base, Sky platform, Stack on last
- **Machines**: Working mystery box, Pack-a-Punch, Perk machine, Der Wunderfizz, Gobblegum machine, Arsenal, Crafting table, Ammo crate
- **Adjust last**: rotate, push away, raise, lower
- **Save / Load** your build per map, Delete last, Delete all

## 🕹️ Old School Zombies
Enable all at once, or pick:
Old-school damage, No armor, Back shield, OG specials & elites, Classic perk prices, 4 perks max (+1 every 20 rounds), Random Wunderfizz, Quick Revive = self revive, No super sprinters, Zombie health past round 60, Classic points, No scrap, One-hit wonder weapons

## ⚙️ Settings
Menu color, Menu on the right, Text mode (no HUD)

## 👥 Co-op
Every player gets the menu. Host: full panel. Other players: text line in the middle of the screen. Only the host needs the mod.

## 📦 Install
Kronos launcher → **Workshop** → **FuzMenu** → Install (or extract the rar into your Kronos folder).
Launch Zombies and press **Aim + Melee**.
